Monthly schedule

Auto-generate rebalances duty groups and fills all three rotation slots in every room for the selected month (2nd years to the ER, 3rd years to the ICU, 1st/2nd years preferred for general rooms; doctors matching the slot's group are picked first, but any group can fill any slot). Everything can still be adjusted manually afterwards.

Room assignments for the month

The ER occupies all of Floor 1 (2nd-year residents only) and the ICU is on Floor 2 (3rd-year residents only). General rooms (Floors 2–5) accept any year; 1st and 2nd years are listed first. Each room has three rotation slots (A/B/C) that cycle Duty → Post-duty → Pre-duty, so every room has someone on Duty every day. A doctor from any group can take any slot — the slot, not their badge group, sets their duty days for that room. Assigning a doctor to a slot reserves them for that floor for the whole month — one floor per doctor (they can still take one-day leave covers elsewhere). On general floors you can also reserve the room for a single group for the month: its three slots (and leave covers) may then only be filled with that group's doctors, one per slot, and those doctors can't hold slots in other rooms (they stay available as one-day leave covers elsewhere).
